Case StudyRaw public data · Nov 2026

Rebuilding the MH370 search from raw physical measurements.The whole public investigation inside an ordinary ABYSSAL case: validated Classic Aero BTO/BFO physics, ERA5 winds, a calibrated Boeing 777 fuel model, 155,000 manoeuvring flights, pixel-measured sonar coverage, 36 years of NOAA drifters, barnacle temperatures, raw IMOS hydrophones, and null tests of seismometers, WSPR, MODIS and Jason-2.

Coverage
120,522 km² measured from Geoscience Australia mosaic pixels (~60 m); 99.67% of the core imaged (0.013% of the prior in gaps).
Result
Fuel and manoeuvre physics close the far south and the north. 43% of the final map lies in a 22,800 km² re-look of the searched core (36.8–38.8°S); 12% in one 2,600 km² escarpment massif near 38.1°S 88.2°E.
