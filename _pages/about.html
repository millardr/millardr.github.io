I'm Rory Millard. I studied Engineering Science at the University of Oxford, graduating with a First in 2026 and ranking first overall in my cohort.

My research has ranged from high-dimensional optimisation and mathematical modelling to Bayesian estimation and machine-learning robustness. My master's project investigated efficient methods for optimising supersonic nozzle geometries.

I'm interested in the places where mathematical ideas meet complicated real systems: artificial intelligence, uncertainty, aerospace, autonomous technology, scientific computing and national security.

This site is where I develop those interests in public. I write notes to clarify ideas, build small experiments when it helps, and sometimes develop them into longer technical work. ABYSSAL is the largest of these: a Bayesian workbench for finding lost things, built to reason honestly about where something might be when searches are imperfect and evidence is messy. It is also an experiment in how one person can build serious, auditable software by directing AI coding agents under written invariants.
