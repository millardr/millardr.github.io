HELLO, I'M RORY

I like questions with
uncomfortable answers.

I studied Engineering Science at the University of Oxford, graduating with First Class Honours and ranking first overall in my cohort. My work has moved between optimisation, probabilistic estimation, machine-learning robustness and physical systems.

What draws me to research is not a single domain so much as a way of thinking: build a model, understand its assumptions, test it against reality, and become suspicious when it seems too certain.

I'm particularly interested in intelligent systems under uncertainty, scientific machine learning, aerospace and space technology, and the ways technical capabilities intersect with geopolitics and security.

What is ABYSSAL?

ABYSSAL began as a probabilistic workbench for search problems: a way to keep an honest account of where uncertainty remains after imperfect observations. The project includes a statistical engine, simulation machinery and a map-based prototype. I'm using it as a springboard for small, public investigations into evidence, model failure and decision-making.

This site is an evolving research notebook, not a claim that any of the work has been peer-reviewed or operationally validated.
