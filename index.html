Featured project

ABYSSAL: a probability map that cannot overclaim

Searches fail in predictable ways: "we covered 80%" heard as "we are 80% done", five retellings of one sighting counted as five witnesses, a failing sensor repeated over the same ground. ABYSSAL replaces those habits with arithmetic. It keeps an honest belief map, updates it after every empty search, explains every change in bits, says where the next hour should go and what it is worth, and records who decided to trust each piece of evidence.

It has grown into a full workstation: an evidence engine with provenance pooling, multi-asset planning with expected value and stopping rules, a live operating picture of aircraft, ships and satellites, and physics-screened detectors for radar wakes, hydrophones and debris rings.
