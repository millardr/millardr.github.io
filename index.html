MH370 · 9M-MRORaw-data Bayesian reanalysis · Nov 2026

MH370: where the evidence still leaves room.Every public line of evidence rebuilt from raw data inside ABYSSAL — Inmarsat BTO/BFO physics, ERA5 winds, pixel-measured sonar mosaics, 36 years of NOAA drifters, raw IMOS hydrophones, and a quantitative audit of the leads that did not hold up.

Satellites
4,400 autopilot configurations plus a 5,040-cell timing-only (BTO-only) scan independently converge on 37.4°S–39.7°S; final BFOs imply ~0.67 g steepening descent (glide weight 0.17).
Sonar
120,522 km² measured directly from Geoscience Australia tiles (~60 m pixels); 99.67% core coverage leaves only 0.013% of the steady prior in gaps.
Null tests
22 island seismometers, 4-hydrophone stacks, 145 MB WSPRnet archive, MODIS sheen drift, and 128 Jason-2 altimeter passes tested against controls.
Revised plan
South extension (39.45°S–40.5°S, 23,395 km², unsearched) holds 36% of the steady posterior (~29% after second-round nulls); core AUV re-look leads if fuel trims Mach 0.84–0.85 tracks.

ABYSSALResearch workbench · ongoing

Probabilistic search and physical signal extraction.A local-first workbench combining log-space Bayesian inference, provenance pooling, 16 physics-based signal engines, search economics, and a live operating picture.

Belief
Double-precision log-space Bayes over space and time; negative searches update belief, repeated echoes count once.
Signals
Sixteen physical detectors (SAR, wakes, sonar, hydroacoustics, magnetics, ocean drift, optical change, viewshed) behind a calibration gate.
Validation
Scored on real public datasets (NOAA buoys, MarineCadastre AIS, BlueTopo wrecks, Sentinel-1/2, ARA San Juan); not yet used in live operations.
