ABYSSALResearch prototype · ongoing

Finding what is lost, with probability that cannot overclaim.A Bayesian workbench for searching for aircraft, ships and people. It keeps an honest map of where the target could be, learns from every empty search, and says where the next hour should go.

Belief
Log-space Bayes over space and time. Empty searches count as evidence; five retellings of one sighting count once.
Signals
Sixteen physics engines (radar, sonar, sound in the ocean, magnetics, drift, satellite imagery and line of sight on land) behind a single calibration gate.
Decisions
Where to search next, what that is worth, and when to stop.
Evidence
Measured on real data against buoys, AIS, nautical charts, real satellite scenes and a real wreck; maths tested against known answers. Not yet used in a live search.
